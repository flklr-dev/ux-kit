**Explanation:** Organize content so people can locate a relevant section and understand its relationship to surrounding information.

**Signals:** A long support page has bold decorative text but no meaningful section structure.

**Interventions:** Use descriptive semantic headings and purposeful order, keeping essential qualifications with the claim.

**Limits:** A rigid heading count or visual scale does not establish comprehensibility.

**Example:** A cancellation guide separates prerequisites, steps and confirmed outcome using headings that can be navigated.

**Validation:** Find a specific answer through visual scanning and heading navigation; check the same meaning in reading order.

**Counterexample:** A promotional heading can make a limitation hard to find even when the words exist below.

**Conflicts:** Balance scan-friendly organization with complete truthful context and semantic structure.

**Evidence boundary:** WAI tutorial and audience guidance inspected; no reading-speed gain or conformance verdict inferred.

**Useful when:** Organize a page so users can locate the next useful information.
