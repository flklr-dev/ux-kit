**Explanation:** Match tone to the action's real consequence and the person's need for control.

**Signals:** A playful failure message hides that work was not saved.

**Interventions:** Say what happened, what remains safe and the useful next step; use reassurance only when supported.

**Limits:** Do not promise recovery, blame users or add humor that obscures a serious outcome.

**Example:** A failed report says the draft remains available and offers retry only if that retention actually holds.

**Validation:** Trigger success, failure and unknown results; compare tone and claims with the stored/effected state.

**Counterexample:** Celebrating a partial result can imply every item succeeded.

**Conflicts:** Truthfulness and actionable recovery override brand personality at consequential moments.

**Evidence boundary:** Status/control heuristics inform this original content practice; no source mandates an exact tone or user reaction.

**Useful when:** Keep serious failure, ordinary guidance and celebration appropriately distinct.
