**Explanation:** Justify account creation by the task rather than making it an unexplained gateway to purchase.

**Signals:** A one-off buyer must build a profile before reviewing an order.

**Interventions:** Offer a fitting guest/reference route when the service can support it, and explain legitimate account needs.

**Limits:** Some ongoing services require identity and account access; do not promise a guest path unsupported by the service.

**Example:** A one-off order can be retrieved through an authorized reference mechanism without a full marketing profile.

**Validation:** Test purchase, receipt retrieval and later help with every offered account route.

**Counterexample:** A guest route that makes the order inaccessible later shifts the barrier to recovery.

**Conflicts:** Balance low effort against actual identity, continuity and security requirements.

**Evidence boundary:** Official account pattern inspected; guest commerce is an explicitly original application, not a universal service requirement or measured sales gain.

**Useful when:** Match account requirements to the service rather than forced registration.
