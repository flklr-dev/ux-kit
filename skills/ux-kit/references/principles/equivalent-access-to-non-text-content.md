**Explanation:** Choose an equivalent according to content purpose and media type; one generic alt text is not every media requirement.

**Signals:** A training video needs its visuals/audio but only a poster description is supplied.

**Interventions:** Use meaningful image alternatives, fitting captions and audio description or applicable media alternatives within the relevant criterion scope.

**Limits:** SC1.1.1 A lists special cases; prerecorded captions1.2.2 A and description1.2.5 AA have distinct conditions. Decorative images can be ignored appropriately.

**Example:** A setup video conveys spoken instruction through captions and essential unspoken visual steps through appropriate description or equivalent content.

**Validation:** Complete the learning task with relevant visual or auditory channel unavailable; inspect accuracy, timing and equivalent purpose.

**Counterexample:** Repeating a filename as alt text or auto-captions without meaningful sound/context may not deliver the intended information.

**Conflicts:** Equivalent task understanding and accurate scope take precedence over decorative consistency.

**Evidence boundary:** Actual criteria/notes inspected; this is a route to scoped checks, not an exhaustive media-accessibility certification.

**Useful when:** Provide useful equivalents for meaningful images, audio and video.
