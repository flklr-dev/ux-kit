**Explanation:** Explain data limitations in terms that change the decision rather than hide them behind precise-looking numbers.

**Signals:** A tiny convenience sample yields a confidently ranked performance score without qualification.

**Interventions:** Show relevant uncertainty, missing coverage and methodological limits with the value; distinguish estimate from observed fact.

**Limits:** Do not invent confidence intervals or attach a percentage without a justified method.

**Example:** A small-site estimate includes its limited denominator and cautions against treating minor rank differences as established.

**Validation:** Trace the estimate/interval to the actual method, check missingness and ask what conclusions readers draw.

**Counterexample:** A footnote cannot fix a headline that states an uncertain ranking as definitive.

**Conflicts:** Clarity and decision relevance govern how much detail appears initially, with inspectable methods available.

**Evidence boundary:** Official uncertainty principles inspected; no estimator, interval formula or causal interpretation is supplied by this entry.

**Useful when:** Represent missingness and uncertainty without false precision.
