**Explanation:** Check whether relevant experience, context and access needs change the task before generalizing a result.

**Signals:** A novice pilot is used to remove a successful expert comparison workflow.

**Interventions:** Define relevant audiences and recruitment/analysis questions; preserve differences and test the affected tasks.

**Limits:** Do not invent personas or mine small subgroups after the result to claim a win; subgroup uncertainty remains.

**Example:** A dispatch redesign separately checks trained operators' comparison work and new staff's terminology difficulties.

**Validation:** Report who was observed, task/context and uncertainty; compare within relevant groups without unsupported population inference.

**Counterexample:** A single average can hide severe barriers for a small relevant audience.

**Conflicts:** Inclusion and task context outweigh a universal minimal interface preference.

**Evidence boundary:** Official participant/group planning guidance inspected; no representative sample or segment-level effectiveness established here.

**Useful when:** Check whether novice and expert needs differ before generalizing.
