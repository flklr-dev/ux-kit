**Explanation:** Make the recurring commitment and relevant change points understandable before acceptance.

**Signals:** A trial appears one-off while recurring billing is disclosed only after enrollment.

**Interventions:** Expose actual charge timing, recurrence and applicable trial/cancellation facts near commitment and in later account access.

**Limits:** Do not invent renewal amounts, dates, rights or guarantees; unknown service facts need resolution.

**Example:** A plan review distinguishes today's charge from the stated later recurrence and links to the actual management path.

**Validation:** Ask users to explain the commitment, then inspect the configured billing states in authorized tests.

**Counterexample:** A clear confirmation after enrollment does not repair hidden recurrence before commitment.

**Conflicts:** Informed choice overrides visual promotion and pressure to accept.

**Evidence boundary:** Staff report identifies concealed recurrence; all product-specific timing and policy must come from the service, not this pattern.

**Useful when:** Explain recurrence, timing and changes before commitment.
