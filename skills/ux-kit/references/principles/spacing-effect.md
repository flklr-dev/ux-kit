**Explanation:** For a skill that needs learning, consider opportunities to practice over time instead of front-loading every instruction.

**Signals:** A complex editor requires remembering a one-time tutorial weeks later.

**Interventions:** Offer short relevant practice and accessible refresher guidance during real use.

**Limits:** Educational study summaries do not establish an ideal product interval or a retention lift.

**Example:** An unfamiliar keyboard command is explained when first useful and remains available in Help on later visits.

**Validation:** Observe later independent task performance, errors and reliance on help after realistic gaps.

**Counterexample:** Repeating an obvious introductory overlay at every launch interrupts skilled work.

**Conflicts:** Adapt help to actual knowledge and user control; do not turn spacing into compulsory repetition.

**Evidence boundary:** Only the publisher's research summary was inspected. Product practice timing is an original qualified hypothesis, not a verified study result.

**Useful when:** Plan repeated learning opportunities for an unfamiliar workflow.
