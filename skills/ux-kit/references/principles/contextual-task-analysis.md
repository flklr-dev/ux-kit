**Explanation:** Establish what people are trying to do in their real setting, with their devices, information and interruptions.

**Signals:** A redesign assumes a clean desktop workflow for field staff using one hand offline.

**Interventions:** Observe relevant activity and constraints, distinguishing user task from the product's current screens.

**Limits:** An observation is scoped to its participants/context; do not generalize every workaround as a universal need.

**Example:** A maintenance audit records how staff reference equipment IDs while wearing gloves before rearranging the capture flow.

**Validation:** Document observed task steps, constraints and exceptions; test proposed changes in a fitting setting.

**Counterexample:** Removing a duplicated paper reference may harm recovery if the digital service is often unavailable.

**Conflicts:** Resolve idealized simplicity against the actual environment and service dependencies.

**Evidence boundary:** Official contextual method inspected; no new field study or population prevalence claimed by this entry.

**Useful when:** Establish the actual task, environment and constraints before redesign.
