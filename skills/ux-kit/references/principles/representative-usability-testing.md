**Explanation:** Observe actual or likely users attempting realistic tasks with a method suited to the question.

**Signals:** A designer demonstrates a polished happy path and calls the feature user-tested.

**Interventions:** Choose relevant participants/tasks, neutral prompts and observable outcomes, including correction/recovery where material.

**Limits:** A small usability study can reveal problems without estimating population rates or proving comparative superiority.

**Example:** Participants submit a realistic request, encounter failure and recover using the available interface without coaching.

**Validation:** Retain task wording, environment, participant fit, observed behavior and completion criteria; separate assistance from independent success.

**Counterexample:** A guided demo can succeed because the moderator supplies every next step.

**Conflicts:** Evidence integrity and inclusion outweigh a desire for a quick pass rate.

**Evidence boundary:** Official task/recruitment guidance inspected; no universal sample count or representative statistical claim adopted.

**Useful when:** Observe relevant people attempting realistic tasks.
