**Explanation:** A boundary can communicate membership. Check whether enclosed information genuinely belongs to the same decision.

**Signals:** A renewal button is boxed with a discount while its recurring terms sit outside.

**Interventions:** Place price, renewal timing and the corresponding action within an understandable decision group.

**Limits:** A border cannot replace clear labels, reading order or accessible relationships.

**Example:** A renewal panel includes the applicable plan, next charge and review action, helping users check the commitment.

**Validation:** Ask which terms apply to each action at supported widths and zoom levels.

**Counterexample:** A large card encloses two unrelated charges and suggests that one action approves both.

**Conflicts:** Use proximity and hierarchy together, preserving distinct commitments.

**Evidence boundary:** The editorial grouping account supports a design hypothesis, not universal interpretation or measured comprehension.

**Useful when:** Group a subscription’s price and terms within a clear boundary.
