**Explanation:** An unfinished-task label is not evidence that reminders will help this audience. Treat reminder usefulness as a hypothesis.

**Signals:** A growth proposal uses incomplete-profile badges to pressure people into optional disclosure.

**Interventions:** Show accurate saved progress and an optional return path; let people dismiss nonessential reminders.

**Limits:** Do not fabricate progress, imply optional work is required or guarantee improved motivation.

**Example:** A report draft shows its real saved state and a Resume action without calling optional photos incomplete.

**Validation:** Test whether reminders help intended resumptions without pressure, mistaken obligation or unwanted notifications.

**Counterexample:** A persistent incomplete badge on an optional health question can coerce disclosure.

**Conflicts:** User control and truthful requirements override an engagement theory.

**Evidence boundary:** Popular account inspected, underlying studies not reviewed; no universal unfinished-task effect is asserted or used to justify pressure.

**Useful when:** Treat unfinished-task reminders as a hypothesis rather than a coercive device.
