**Explanation:** Clear feedback names the result and useful next step; apply that same specificity to a control's promise.

**Useful when:** Save, send, pay, cancel, retry and destructive-action labels.

**Signals:** 'Submit' saves a private draft; 'Cancel' merely closes continuing work; 'Retry' repeats successful actions.

**Interventions:** Use a short verb/object matching actual scope and state. Add consequence text only where it changes the decision.

**Limits:** This label method is an original application, not a normative wording requirement. Preserve established terminology when it is understood.

**Example:** 'Save draft' for an unfinished private report that remains editable.

**Validation:** Ask representative users what they expect before pressing; compare their expectation with observed behavior.

**Evidence boundary:** WAI notification guidance informs this original action-label method; it does not prescribe the exact words on every control.

**Counterexample:** A familiar domain term may be clearer than a longer generic phrase for experienced operators.

**Conflicts:** Resolve short labels against clarity of actual scope; keep concise consequence text nearby where needed.
