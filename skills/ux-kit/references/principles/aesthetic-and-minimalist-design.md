**Explanation:** Reduce irrelevant competition while retaining information necessary to complete and check the task.

**Signals:** Promotional cards crowd out the useful comparison table.

**Interventions:** Remove or reduce unrelated emphasis, maintaining the data and controls users need.

**Limits:** Aesthetic minimalism is not a quota on fields, density or visible options.

**Example:** An expert dashboard removes a campaign banner while keeping all useful comparison columns.

**Validation:** Observe the same comparison tasks and inspect newly hidden information or controls.

**Counterexample:** Turning every row into a sparse card can force memory and repeated navigation.

**Conflicts:** Useful expert complexity and accessible operation outweigh a sparse visual style.

**Evidence boundary:** First-party heuristic and its necessary-information boundary inspected; no universal minimal layout is prescribed.

**Useful when:** Remove irrelevant competition while preserving useful information.
