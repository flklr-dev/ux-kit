**Explanation:** Let people understand the real payable total before they commit.

**Signals:** A prominent subtotal competes with unexplained charges appearing only at the final action.

**Interventions:** Show known mandatory components and total clearly; explain any amount that remains dependent on an unresolved choice.

**Limits:** Use actual product facts; do not invent taxes, fees or jurisdiction-specific obligations.

**Example:** A ticket checkout updates the total beside the commitment action when a delivery option changes.

**Validation:** Change quantities/options and compare displayed components, total and charged amount in an authorized test.

**Counterexample:** A low headline price is misleading if unavoidable costs remain obscured.

**Conflicts:** Resolve visual emphasis and conversion goals in favor of informed commitment.

**Evidence boundary:** Report identifies concealed-price patterns; this is original transparency guidance, not current-law or refund-rights advice.

**Useful when:** Reveal the actual total before a purchase commitment.
