**Explanation:** Review significant moments and endings while checking the whole journey; remembered experience and live task success differ.

**Signals:** A team celebrates checkout completion while ignoring repeated failures earlier in payment.

**Interventions:** Make consequential transitions and confirmed outcomes understandable, retaining recovery throughout.

**Limits:** Do not add unpleasant delay or neglect middle-task failures to manipulate later memory.

**Example:** An order confirmation explains the actual outcome and next steps after a recoverable payment flow.

**Validation:** Observe failure recovery and completion, then separately ask about remembered experience.

**Counterexample:** A delightful confetti ending cannot repair a duplicate charge.

**Conflicts:** Correctness, truthful confirmation and complete-task evidence outrank a remembered-impression theory.

**Evidence boundary:** Popular account inspected; original peak/end experiments not reviewed and no business effect inferred.

**Useful when:** Improve important moments and endings without neglecting intervening failures.
