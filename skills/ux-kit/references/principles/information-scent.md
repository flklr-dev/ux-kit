**Explanation:** A destination label and its context should let users predict whether following it serves their current question.

**Signals:** Several unrelated links say More or use internal team names.

**Interventions:** Use specific task or content labels with relevant context and truthful destinations.

**Limits:** A label's usefulness depends on the audience and information need.

**Example:** A billing page links to Download invoice beside the relevant transaction instead of an unexplained document icon.

**Validation:** Ask users where they would go for a stated need and compare their prediction with the destination.

**Counterexample:** Clickbait can attract a click while delivering the wrong information.

**Conflicts:** Resolve brevity against meaningful destination context and established user vocabulary.

**Evidence boundary:** First-party synthesis inspected; no original foraging experiment or guaranteed navigation outcome is claimed.

**Useful when:** Make link labels predict a destination’s usefulness.
