**Explanation:** Pressure cues must correspond to real relevant facts, rather than fabricated urgency or popularity.

**Signals:** A timer restarts after expiry or a stock warning ignores actual inventory.

**Interventions:** Remove unsupported cues; show genuine availability or deadlines with accurate scope when useful.

**Limits:** Do not assert a guaranteed conversion effect or invent live demand data.

**Example:** A booking page displays an actual held-reservation expiry and explains what expires, without pretending the whole offer disappears.

**Validation:** Refresh, wait past expiry and compare the cue with authoritative availability and actual consequences.

**Counterexample:** A permanent countdown can pressure a person even when nothing changes at zero.

**Conflicts:** Honesty and informed review override a motivation tactic.

**Evidence boundary:** Report classifications inspected; product checks are original and do not establish legal compliance or a measured persuasion effect.

**Useful when:** Avoid manufactured pressure and unverifiable endorsements.
