**Explanation:** Keep equivalent actions predictable within the product and use appropriate external conventions when their meaning fits.

**Signals:** Save preserves a draft on one screen but publishes externally on another.

**Interventions:** Reuse established behavior for equivalent roles; signal a consequential difference and respect target-platform conventions.

**Limits:** Familiarity is not a reason to copy inaccessible or deceptive behavior.

**Example:** Related editors use Save draft consistently, while Send identifies its external consequence.

**Validation:** Exercise the same task across screens and check whether expected and actual effects align.

**Counterexample:** Copying a competitor's obstructed cancellation preserves a convention while harming control.

**Conflicts:** Truthful action meaning and scoped access requirements override consistency for its own sake.

**Evidence boundary:** NN/g internal/external consistency and the popular Jakob account were inspected; no original familiarity effect or universal transfer claim is asserted.

**Useful when:** Give the same action predictable meaning across screens.
