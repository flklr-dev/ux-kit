**Explanation:** Investigate avoidable process overhead without treating a humorous organizational dictum as a time law.

**Signals:** A long form asks repeatedly for known information and then adds a short deadline.

**Interventions:** Remove redundant steps and support appropriate reuse of accurate data; let the real task determine needed time.

**Limits:** Arbitrary urgency can exclude people or induce mistakes; a deadline is not a UX improvement by itself.

**Example:** A renewal reuses current contact details with correction instead of requiring retyping under a timer.

**Validation:** Compare completion, corrections and timeout recovery with real task conditions.

**Counterexample:** A faster forced submission may prevent checking an important charge.

**Conflicts:** Accuracy, user control and access needs govern timing policy.

**Evidence boundary:** Origins section identifies a humorous essay; no scientific task-duration prediction is asserted.

**Useful when:** Investigate unnecessary workflow overhead without imposing arbitrary deadlines.
