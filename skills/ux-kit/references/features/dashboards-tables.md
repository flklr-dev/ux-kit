# Dashboards Tables

Identify the decision and comparisons users make. Keep successful expert density and controls while checking the meaning and provenance of values.

**Inspect:** What are the unit, denominator, period and baseline? Is missing/unavailable different from zero? Are sorts semantic and selection scopes exact? Does a chart encode the relevant relationship and provide equivalent access?

**Exercise:** Recompute samples, inject missing/zero/stale data, sort ties/nulls, combine filters and select across pages. Inspect actual affected IDs for bulk work. Test keyboard, focus, relevant resize/reflow exceptions and nonvisual alternatives.

**Preserve and resolve:** More whitespace or fewer columns is not an automatic improvement. Preserve demonstrated comparisons while clarifying ambiguous units or harmful missing-data substitutions. A visually simpler chart cannot validate an unsupported ranking.
