# Settings Privacy

Inspect what each control changes, who it affects, where the state persists and the service data-use facts. Preserve a coherent existing settings model.

**Inspect:** Do defaults and labels communicate real scope? Does Off stop the promised behavior? Can a person discover changed settings and reverse an appropriate choice? Are optional permissions/notifications distinguished from core service access?

**Exercise:** Change settings, cancel/dismiss, reopen/restart and inspect real behavior; check denied/revoked permissions and actual shared/local scope. Exercise dialog focus and keyboard paths separately from visual appearance.

**Preserve and resolve:** Consistency must not conceal different ownership or consequences. An easy-looking toggle is harmful if it promises privacy the service does not provide; show only verified policy and effective controls.

**Select references:** Start with one or two entries for the unresolved decision; this list is a lookup, not a required reading bundle.

- [Mental Models](../principles/mental-models.md) — Make a scheduling feature match users’ expectations of time and ownership.
- [Consistency and Standards](../principles/consistency-and-standards.md) — Give the same action predictable meaning across screens.
- [Name the Action’s Real Outcome](../principles/action-labels.md) — Use a label matching what the action will actually do.
- [AI Data Use and Disclosure](../principles/ai-data-use-and-disclosure.md) — Explain relevant data handling before a consequential use of assistance.
- [Agency-Preserving Defaults](../principles/agency-preserving-defaults.md) — Choose defaults that support informed correction and choice.
- [Notification Consent and Control](../principles/notification-consent-and-control.md) — Support relevant notifications with understandable controls.
- [Closing, Cancellation and Undo](../principles/cancellation.md) — Communicate whether work stops or merely becomes hidden.
- [Modal Focus Behavior](../principles/dialog-focus.md) — Support deliberate initial focus, containment and return.
- [Figure–Ground Separation](../principles/figure-ground-separation.md) — Distinguish foreground dialogs and background content.
- [Progressive Disclosure](../principles/progressive-disclosure.md) — Reveal advanced settings without hiding frequently required controls.
- [Occam’s Razor](../principles/occams-razor.md) — Prefer an explanation or design with fewer unsupported assumptions.
- [Match Between System and Real World](../principles/match-between-system-and-real-world.md) — Use concepts and ordering users recognize from their task.

**Report:** State the observed difficulty, smallest fitting change, tradeoff and actual checks. Separate source inspection, execution, browser/device evidence and human research. Do not invent a finding or claim an unmeasured benefit.
