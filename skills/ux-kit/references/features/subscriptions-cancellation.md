# Subscriptions Cancellation

Identify actual recurrence, service end timing and the available cancellation operation. Distinguish stopping renewal, immediate termination, pausing and account/data deletion.

**Inspect:** Are commitment and management terms reachable before acceptance and later? Does each action say what it actually changes? Can a user decline optional retention without losing the cancellation path?

**Exercise:** Walk the complete start/manage/end path, decline offers, retry only within confirmed scope and return to the authoritative account state. Check keyboard, labels and the actual effective result.

**Preserve and resolve:** A reversible Pause and End renewal can be separate useful choices; do not substitute one secretly. Preserve access or data according to the actual policy, and do not promise a refund or immediate end without evidence.

**Select references:** Start with one or two entries for the unresolved decision; this list is a lookup, not a required reading bundle.

- [Subscription and Renewal Clarity](../principles/subscription-and-renewal-clarity.md) — Explain recurrence, timing and changes before commitment.
- [Cancellation Parity](../principles/cancellation-parity.md) — Make ending a service understandable and reasonably reachable.
- [Closing, Cancellation and Undo](../principles/cancellation.md) — Communicate whether work stops or merely becomes hidden.
- [Name the Action’s Real Outcome](../principles/action-labels.md) — Use a label matching what the action will actually do.
- [Common Region](../principles/common-region.md) — Group a subscription’s price and terms within a clear boundary.
- [Completed, Failed and Unknown Outcomes](../principles/partial-outcomes.md) — Reconcile partial outcomes before retrying consequential work.

**Report:** State the observed difficulty, smallest fitting change, tradeoff and actual checks. Separate source inspection, execution, browser/device evidence and human research. Do not invent a finding or claim an unmeasured benefit.
