# Usability And Control

Inspect the action promise, actual service effect and recovery. Preserve useful expert comparison. For consequential actions define empty, pending, overlapping, complete, failed and unknown behavior; exercise effects rather than relying on a disabled control. Consistency cannot justify deceptive or inaccessible conventions.

- [Closing, Cancellation and Undo](../principles/cancellation.md): Communicate whether work stops or merely becomes hidden.
- [Preserve Useful Expert Complexity](../principles/expert-complexity.md): Retain comparison information and efficient established workflows.
- [Visibility of System Status](../principles/visibility-of-system-status.md): Make saves and background work understandable.
- [Match Between System and Real World](../principles/match-between-system-and-real-world.md): Use concepts and ordering users recognize from their task.
- [Consistency and Standards](../principles/consistency-and-standards.md): Give the same action predictable meaning across screens.
- [Error Prevention](../principles/error-prevention.md): Prevent a consequential mistake before committing it.
- [Aesthetic and Minimalist Design](../principles/aesthetic-and-minimalist-design.md): Remove irrelevant competition while preserving useful information.
- [Action-State Guards](../principles/action-state-guards.md): Handle pending, overlapping and empty actions without duplicate effects.
- [Postel’s Law and Input Tolerance](../principles/postels-law-and-input-tolerance.md): Accept harmless formatting variation while rejecting ambiguous or unsafe values.
- [Mode Awareness and Mode Errors](../principles/mode-awareness-and-mode-errors.md): Show whether the same control currently navigates, selects or edits.

Preserve successful behavior. Prefer scoped accessibility and platform requirements over a conflicting heuristic; report evidence limits.
