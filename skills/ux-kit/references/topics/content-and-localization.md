# Content And Localization

Check audience vocabulary, semantic structure and the real outcome promised by labels. Treat translation as complete messages with locale-sensitive values and mixed-script behavior. Inspect real language/layout combinations; pseudolocalization and formatters cannot prove translation quality.

Preserve accurate familiar names, headings, action labels and established tone. For a change, identify the misunderstanding it resolves and use concise natural wording consistent with the rest of the product. Explain an unusual consequence beside its decision; do not turn ordinary labels into repeated instructions. Separate the **current state/value**, **available action** and **consequence**: an access column needs actual access information, not the same edit instruction in every row.

Price wording must match completeness: a partial amount includes the computed sum of known selected charges, explicitly names fees still missing, and is not a final payable total. Show that sum rather than only listing components for the user to add. Keep a familiar “Total” or “Total amount” when the amount is complete; choose an understandable qualified label for an incomplete amount rather than obscure jargon or hiding the known additions. Reconcile the visible breakdown and displayed sum before deciding on the label.

Match a button to the actual next step. A supplied review-then-confirm flow can say “Review”; a supplied immediate commitment must describe that commitment. Propose an extra review stage only with an explicit purpose and tradeoff; distinguish that proposal from existing behavior. Group related terms/privacy information when appropriate and keep optional marketing independent; resolve actual consent/age/data requirements rather than adding a checkbox or assertion for each sentence by default.

For state-dependent promises such as eligibility, availability, deadlines or completion, check the displayed message together with the calculated value. Inspect the full returned copy in each relevant state; correcting an amount or comparison alone may leave an expired promise visible. At a supplied policy boundary, compare just before, exactly at and after it in each locale. Specify which complete message appears, disappears or changes, preserving placeholder contracts. Use only supplied product rules; this is a content/logic check, not legal approval.
