# Research And Evaluation

Begin with a question and evidence suited to it. Keep observed behavior, statements, heuristic judgments and causal estimates distinct. Recruit relevant audiences; preserve provenance, uncertainty, guardrails and planned inference/stopping. A local synthetic screen is not representative usability or business-effect proof.

- [Bound Conclusions to Evidence](../principles/evidence.md): Distinguish observed interaction from assumptions about an artifact.
- [Contextual Task Analysis](../principles/contextual-task-analysis.md): Establish the actual task, environment and constraints before redesign.
- [Representative Usability Testing](../principles/representative-usability-testing.md): Observe relevant people attempting realistic tasks.
- [Observed Behavior and Self-Report](../principles/observed-behavior-and-self-report.md): Keep stated preference distinct from demonstrated task performance.
- [Heuristic Evaluation Calibration](../principles/heuristic-evaluation-calibration.md): Separate problem impact from evaluator confidence.
- [Triangulation](../principles/triangulation.md): Use complementary evidence to investigate a consequential uncertainty.
- [Causal Claims and Experiments](../principles/causal-claims-and-experiments.md): Distinguish a plausible design improvement from a proven causal outcome.
- [Experiment Stopping and Repeated Looks](../principles/experiment-stopping-and-repeated-looks.md): Choose and respect a defensible experiment analysis protocol.
- [Metrics and Guardrails](../principles/metrics-and-guardrails.md): Measure task success alongside regressions and harmful tradeoffs.
- [Audience and Experience Segmentation](../principles/audience-and-experience-segmentation.md): Check whether novice and expert needs differ before generalizing.

Preserve successful behavior. Prefer scoped accessibility and platform requirements over a conflicting heuristic; report evidence limits.
