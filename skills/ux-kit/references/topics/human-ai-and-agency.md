# Human Ai And Agency

Preserve originals, editable suggestions and appropriate explicit review. Inspect actual target scope and effects, not model narration. Communicate useful uncertainty and evidence; allow correction, fallback and effective controls without unnecessary approval ceremonies. Bulk scope resolves to the data entry; automation-bias screening resolves to the cognition entry.

- [Completed, Failed and Unknown Outcomes](../principles/partial-outcomes.md): Reconcile partial outcomes before retrying consequential work.
- [Consequential AI Review](../principles/ai-review.md): Keep intended targets and consequential AI actions reviewable.
- [Meaningful AI Uncertainty](../principles/meaningful-ai-uncertainty.md): Explain uncertainty in terms relevant to the user’s decision.
- [AI Provenance and Evidence](../principles/ai-provenance-and-evidence.md): Make source and generation provenance useful to inspect.
- [Correction and Override](../principles/correction-and-override.md): Let people correct the system without losing their own work.
- [Human Escalation and Fallback](../principles/human-escalation-and-fallback.md): Provide a workable alternative when assistance cannot complete the task.
- [AI Data Use and Disclosure](../principles/ai-data-use-and-disclosure.md): Explain relevant data handling before a consequential use of assistance.
- [Agency-Preserving Defaults](../principles/agency-preserving-defaults.md): Choose defaults that support informed correction and choice.

Preserve successful behavior. Prefer scoped accessibility and platform requirements over a conflicting heuristic; report evidence limits.
