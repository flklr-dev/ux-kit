# Accessibility And Inclusion

Use the actual criterion, level, scope and exceptions. Keyboard, focus, semantics, color, resize and media checks address different properties; a scan or screenshot cannot certify a full task. Prefer sound native controls, then verify integration and actual interactions with appropriate tools.

- [Target Size: Scope and Units](../principles/targets.md): Assess effective hit bounds under the actual platform or standard.
- [Modal Focus Behavior](../principles/dialog-focus.md): Support deliberate initial focus, containment and return.
- [Meaningful Status Announcements](../principles/status.md): Expose important updates without needless interruption.
- [Keyboard Operation and No Traps](../principles/keyboard-operation-and-no-traps.md): Complete relevant tasks with the keyboard.
- [Visible and Unobscured Focus](../principles/visible-and-unobscured-focus.md): Keep the currently focused control identifiable.
- [Semantic Name, Role and Value](../principles/semantic-name-role-and-value.md): Expose an interactive control’s meaning and state.
- [Contrast and Color Independence](../principles/contrast-and-color-independence.md): Make information perceivable without relying on color alone.
- [Text Resize and Reflow](../principles/text-resize-and-reflow.md): Keep content and actions available when text or viewport scales.
- [Reduced Motion and Pause Controls](../principles/reduced-motion-and-pause-controls.md): Respect relevant motion preferences and user control of moving content.
- [Equivalent Access to Non-Text Content](../principles/equivalent-access-to-non-text-content.md): Provide useful equivalents for meaningful images, audio and video.

Preserve successful behavior. Prefer scoped accessibility and platform requirements over a conflicting heuristic; report evidence limits.
